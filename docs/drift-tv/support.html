Drift TV FAQ

Simple answers for setup, playback, audio, and wind-down features.

How do I connect Plex?

Open Settings in Drift TV, choose Plex, and enter your Plex server address and token. The app stores those details locally on your device.

Does Drift TV support Jellyfin?

Yes. Jellyfin support is included. Add your Jellyfin server address and authentication details in Settings.

How does the sleep timer work?

Set a timer before playback or during a mood screen. As the timer ends, Drift TV gradually dims the screen and fades the audio.

Can I change the audio source?

Yes. In Settings, change the audio source URL to your preferred stream. The default ambient streams come from NullRecords Radio.
