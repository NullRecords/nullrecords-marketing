Built for low-friction evenings

  • Sleep timer with gradual dim and fade.
  • Per-episode sound leveling for easier late-night watching.
  • Works with your own Plex and Jellyfin libraries.
  • No account required for Drift TV itself.

Your library, NullRecords atmosphere

Video comes from your own Plex or Jellyfin server. Ambient, mood, and retro audio streams come from NullRecords Radio at radio.nullrecords.com.
