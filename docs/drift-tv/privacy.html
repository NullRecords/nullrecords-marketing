Privacy Policy

This policy applies to Drift TV v1.0.0 and later on Apple TV and Android/Google TV.

Summary

Drift TV collects no personal data. The app has no analytics, no Drift TV user accounts, and no advertising trackers.

Local device storage

Plex and Jellyfin server addresses, authentication tokens, and app settings are stored locally on your device only. NullRecords does not receive or store those details.

Media playback

Video streams directly from your own Plex or Jellyfin servers. Ambient, mood, and retro audio streams from NullRecords Radio at radio.nullrecords.com.

Optional AI mood scoring

If you choose to enable AI mood scoring and provide your own OpenAI API key, Drift TV may send library titles or metadata to OpenAI's API under your own key. This feature is off by default and only runs when you initiate it.

Third parties

NullRecords does not sell or share Drift TV user data with third parties. Because Drift TV does not collect personal data, there is no personal data for NullRecords to sell.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact team@nullrecords.com.
